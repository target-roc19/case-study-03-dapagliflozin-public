_index.en

D3_PERSONS

contains the cleaned version of PERSONS, where death date is reconstituted as date

D3_OBSPERIODS

contains the spells all the continuous spells of observation period of each person. in CPRD two versions of this dataset are generate, where the records are stratified per category: category_subpop = PC means that during the period the person is observed in primary care; category_subpop = PC_HOSP means that during the period the person is observed both in primary care and in hospital

output_spells_category_meaning_set

D3_selection_criteria_from_D3_PERSONS_to_D3_ENTRY_FLOWCHART

contains the exclusion criteria to go from D3_PERSONS to the persons having at least one potential episode of interest (D3_PERSONS_WITH_BASE_INCLUSION). Specifically we sected all persons from D3_PERSONS who enter the attrition table. All individuals registered at any time between 2012 and 2024

D3_ENTRY_FLOWCHART

contains the persons complying with the first group of eligibility criteria

D5_DIAGNOSTIC_CLEAN_LAB

Counts of records of laboratory values that exited in specific catagories of the cleaning function CleanLabValues https://github.com/VAC4EU/CleanLabValues

D5_DIAGNOSTIC_CLEAN_LAB_masked

Masked counts of records of laboratory values that exited in specific catagories of the cleaning function CleanLabValues https://github.com/VAC4EU/CleanLabValues

D3_EGFR

values of EGFR obtaines either directly or from calculation based on creatinine serum

D3_covariate_simple_TD

D3_covariate_complex_TD

D3_EPISODES_STUDY_DRUGS

episode of treatments with DPP4I or dapagliflozin

D5_check_study_drugs_duration

Counts of records with a each documented duration, observed on each medicinal product/ATC

D5_check_study_drugs_duration_masked

Counts of records with a each documented duration, observed on each medicinal product/ATC and masked

D3_ELIGIBILITY_EPISODE

Attrition Table, calculated at episode level (implementin inclusione criteria 7.3.2 of the study protocol)

D3_ELIGIBILITY

Attrition Table, calculated at person level
